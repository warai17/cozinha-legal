BuenoWarner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

BuenoWarner is a recipe and meal planning app built for a single private household. It is operated by an individual developer, not a company. This policy describes what the app stores, where it goes, and who can see it.

Information the app stores

  • Account information. Your email address, used to sign you in and to connect you to your household. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth using either an email and password or Google sign-in.
  • Content you create. Recipes, ingredients, instructions, tags, meal plans, shopping lists, pantry items, cooking notes, and the kitchen preferences the Chef keeps for you.
  • Photos you choose to import. If you add a recipe from a photo, that image is sent for text extraction and is not retained after the recipe is created.
  • Location, only while Shop Mode is open. If you grant permission, your coordinates are used once to look up supermarkets near you so the app can guess which store you are in. Your location is never stored and is not linked to your account.

Services the app relies on

BuenoWarner uses a small number of outside services to work. Each receives only what it needs for the feature you are using.

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in. Your account and all content you create are stored here. Privacy policy.
  • Anthropic powers the Chef and the guidance you get in Shop Mode. Your Chef messages, a summary of your saved recipes, and photos you attach in the Chef are sent when you use that tab; a photo goes to Anthropic as an image if a text reading of it is not ready yet. Shop Mode sends the names of items on your shopping list so it can sort them into supermarket sections and answer "Where is this?" questions. Privacy policy.
  • Google provides Google sign-in, and the Gemini model that reads recipe photos, pasted text, and web pages you import a recipe from. Privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap supplies the nearby supermarket lookup used by Shop Mode. Privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, or behavioural profiling. The app contains no analytics SDK.
  • Your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

Who can see your content

Recipes, plans, and lists are visible to members of your household and to nobody else. Access is enforced at the database level through row level security, so one household cannot read another household's data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your content is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual recipes, lists, and notes at any time from inside the app. The fastest way to delete your account and everything attached to it: open BuenoWarner, go to Settings, and tap Delete account. It happens immediately and cannot be undone. If you can no longer sign in, contact the address below instead.

Children

BuenoWarner is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.
