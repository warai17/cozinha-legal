Delete your Cozinha account

Last updated: 23 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your account for Cozinha, the recipe and meal planning app, and exactly what happens to your data when you do.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to waraidev@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my Cozinha account.
  3. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Cozinha. This is how the request is verified, so a request sent from any other address cannot be actioned.

Requests are handled within 30 days. You will get a reply confirming the deletion once it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and the email address attached to it.
  • Your sign-in credentials, including any Google sign-in link.
  • Your user ID, and your membership of any household.
  • Your name as the author on recipes and cooking log entries.

What is kept, and why

A household in Cozinha is shared between its members. If someone else is still in your household, the recipes, meal plans, shopping lists, pantry, and cooking notes stay with them. They were shared content, not yours alone, and deleting your account must not empty the other person's recipe book. Your authorship link to that content is removed.

If you are the only member of your household, then the household and everything in it is deleted along with your account. Nothing is kept.

You can also ask for the whole household and its contents to be deleted rather than just your account. Say so in your email and it will be done.

Retention period

Once deletion is carried out, your data is removed from the live database immediately. Encrypted database backups may still contain it for up to 30 days, after which those backups expire and the data is gone permanently. Backups are never used for any purpose other than restoring the service after a failure.
